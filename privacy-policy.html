Information We Collect

When you use Kolisa, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

a. Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Phone number or email address and password.
  • KYC Documents: National Identification Number (NIN), Bank Verification Number (BVN), Driver’s License, Passport, Utility Bill.
  • Transaction Details: Information related to money transfers, bill payments, airtime/data purchases, and loan/overdraft activities.
  • Beneficiary List: Saved contact information of recipients for easy transfers.

b. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Mobile device ID, operating system, IP address, and app usage analytics.
  • Log Information: Date and time of access, app features used, and performance logs.
  • Location Data: Approximate or precise device location depending on permissions, used for security and fraud prevention.

c. Communication & Notification Preferences

  • Notification Services: SMS, email, and push notifications via Firebase.
  • User Preferences: Your chosen communication method(s).

How We Use Your Information

  • Account creation and user authentication.
  • Processing transactions including money transfers, bill payments, airtime/data purchases, and loan/overdraft services.
  • Verifying your identity and complying with Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations.
  • Sending important notifications about your transactions, account activity, and service updates.
  • Customizing your experience and improving our services.
  • Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraud, security breaches, and misuse.
  • Using location data to enhance account security, detect unusual activity, protect against fraud, and comply with financial regulatory requirements.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. However, we may share your data in the following cases:

  • With Service Providers: For payments, KYC verification, location-based fraud detection, and notification delivery.
  • For Legal Compliance: To meet regulatory requirements, respond to lawful requests, or protect against fraud.
  • Business Transfers: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including encryption, secure servers, and access controls, to protect your data.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Your Privacy Choices

  • Notification Preferences: Choose SMS, email, or push notifications.
  • Access & Update: Update details in app settings.
  • Location Permissions: Manage via device settings (restricting may limit security features).
  • Account Deletion: Request account closure and deletion subject to legal requirements.

Third-Party Services

Kolisa uses Firebase for notifications and integrates with third parties for payments, KYC, and fraud detection. These services have their own privacy policies.

Children’s Privacy

Kolisa is not intended for individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the app or your chosen notification method.
